Pixelmake is not an AQA centre. Courses are mapped to Unit Award Scheme outcomes so that a registered centre can award them. Home educators can go through a registered centre (many small providers offer this for a yearly fee) or simply use the materials without certification.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is the AQA Unit Award Scheme?

A way for AQA, England's largest exam board, to recognise small steps of learning without exams. Every completed unit earns an official certificate listing exactly what the learner can now do. It's widely used in SEN settings, alternative provision and home education.

Can we use the courses at home?

Yes. Everything works at the kitchen table. If you want the AQA certificate at the end, the evidence needs to be checked and submitted by a registered centre.

Do we need a GraviTrax set for the marble-run course?

Yes. The Starter Set covers Levels 1 to 3. Action tiles and expansion sets open up Levels 4 and 5, though the cards are written so you can swap in whatever pieces you own.

What format are the files?

Printables come as PDF and editable Word (.docx). The Start Your Business workbook is a single file that opens in any browser and works offline, so it runs on school tablets without an install.

What does "centre licence" mean?

One price covers your whole centre, or your whole household, with as many learners as you like. Please don't pass files between organisations; a second licence costs less than a coffee a week.

What's your refund policy?

Digital files can't be returned once downloaded, so do read the full contents list first. If a file is faulty or won't open, email us and we'll put it right.
